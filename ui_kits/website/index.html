Who We Are

A partner built for the long term, not the next quarter.

Quanta Capital Holdings Inc. is a Canadian investment firm and long-term holding company headquartered in Toronto. We acquire established, profitable lower-middle-market businesses and hold them indefinitely, free of the artificial five-to-seven-year exit cycle that defines traditional private equity.

Our mission is straightforward: partner with founders, families, and management teams to extend the legacy of great Canadian businesses. We bring patient capital, hands-on operating expertise, and a permanent home for companies whose owners are ready for their next chapter without surrendering everything they have built.

When you partner with Quanta, you gain more than a buyer. You gain a committed team with skin in the game, a clear plan for compounding growth, and a partnership measured in decades, not quarters.

What We Look For

Our acquisition criteria

We invest in proven Canadian businesses with strong fundamentals, defensible market positions, and clear pathways for compounding growth. We are sector-agnostic and focused on durable, resilient companies.

Financial profile

  • EBITDA: CAD $400K to $5M
  • Track record: at least five years of consistent profitability
  • Cash flow: stable, recurring, or contracted revenue preferred

Business characteristics

  • Market position: defensible niche, brand strength, or technical moat
  • Customer base: diversified, loyal, repeat buyers
  • Management: capable team willing to remain through transition
  • Growth runway: operational, geographic, or product-line opportunities

Geography & ownership

  • Headquarters: Canada, with primary focus on Ontario, Quebec, British Columbia, and Alberta
  • Sellers: founders planning succession, families exploring exit, retiring owners
  • Corporate carve-outs: non-core divisions of larger companies
  • Cross-border: Canadian subsidiaries of US or international parents

Deal structures we support

  • Majority equity: control acquisitions where we are the long-term home
  • Management buyouts: partnering with existing leadership
  • Family succession: structured handovers preserving legacy
  • Recapitalizations: liquidity for owners who want to stay invested
Where We Invest

Industries we serve

We focus on essential, time-tested sectors of the Canadian economy where operational excellence (not financial engineering) drives long-term value.

Manufacturing & Industrials

Precision manufacturers, custom fabrication, contract assembly, and engineered components serving Canadian and North American markets.

Distribution & Logistics

Niche distributors, value-added wholesalers, third-party logistics, last-mile delivery, and specialty supply chain operators.

Business Services

Facility services, professional services, technical services, route-based services, and recurring B2B service providers.

Healthcare Services

Multi-site clinical operations, ancillary healthcare services, dental and veterinary platforms, and healthcare staffing.

Construction & Specialty Trades

Commercial trades, infrastructure services, building products, and recurring maintenance for institutional and commercial clients.

Consumer & Retail

Branded consumer products, multi-unit retail concepts, direct-to-consumer e-commerce, and household-name regional brands.

Food & Beverage

Specialty food producers, beverage brands, food service operators, and craft manufacturers with strong regional followings.

Technology-Enabled Services

Vertical SaaS, business process outsourcing, managed IT services, and data-led service businesses serving SMB or mid-market customers.
